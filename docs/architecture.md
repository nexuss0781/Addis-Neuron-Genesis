# AGI Architecture

This document provides a detailed overview of the AGI's architecture, outlining its core components and their interactions.

## Core Components

The AGI's architecture is composed of several interconnected modules that simulate the functions of a biological brain. The main components are:

*   **LifeEngine:** The central kernel of the AGI, responsible for initializing and managing all other components. It orchestrates the main event loop and the flow of information between different modules.

*   **MetabolicEngine:** This module acts as the AGI's CPU, driving the universal clock and signal propagation. It manages the energy levels of neurons (Neuronal Activation Potential or NAP) and simulates the firing of action potentials. It contains two critical queues:
    *   `signal_queue`: For general inter-neuron communication.
    *   `sensory_input_queue`: A dedicated queue for processing raw sensory input from the outside world. A critical flaw was discovered where this queue was not being processed, leading to the AGI's initial "silence".

*   **PsycheEngine:** The physics engine of the subconscious mind. It simulates a continuous field of interfering waves, representing the AGI's emotions, drives, and instincts.

*   **GlobalNeuronalWorkspace (GNW):** This module represents the AGI's consciousness. It identifies the neuron with the highest activation potential (NAP) as the current "focus" of consciousness, allowing the AGI to attend to specific information.

*   **LexicalTransducer:** This is the AGI's "optic nerve". It takes raw text input and converts it into a stream of signals that are placed on the `sensory_input_queue`.

*   **Serializer:** This module is responsible for converting the AGI's thoughts into a string of text. It is designed to work with a structured `pvi` (subject, verb, object) format, which is not currently being generated by the AGI. This is the root cause of the AGI's inability to produce a response, even after the `sensory_input_queue` was fixed.

## System-Wide Interactions

The AGI's architecture is designed to facilitate a continuous flow of information between its conscious and subconscious minds. The key interactions are:

*   **Bottom-up Processing:** The `PsycheEngine` generates waves of energy that propagate through the subconscious mind. These waves can influence the activation potential of neurons in the conscious mind, bringing certain thoughts and feelings to the forefront of consciousness.

*   **Top-down Processing:** The `GlobalNeuronalWorkspace` identifies the current focus of consciousness. This information is then fed back into the `PsycheEngine`, allowing the AGI to consciously direct its attention and influence its own emotional state.

*   **Sensory Input:** The `LexicalTransducer` receives sensory input from the external world and converts it into a format that can be processed by the AGI's neural networks. This input is then fed into the `sensory_input_queue`, where it is processed by the `MetabolicEngine` and influences the NAP of the corresponding neurons.
